# RNA-seq atlas: study admission and interpretation

Decision record: 14 October 2026. This document records which study-level evidence may enter the *S. mansoni* RNA-seq atlas and why. It is **not** an atlas build, a cross-study DESeq2 analysis, or a replacement for each study's result package. The atlas remains a descriptive comparison of independently analysed studies; a contrast is interpreted only within its originating study.

`INCLUDE` means the specified evidence is scientifically approved for an atlas version. It does not assert that the atlas or every newly approved artifact has already been published. Technical workflow completion, biological interpretability, and atlas admission are distinct decisions. The canonical input for an approved contrast is its reviewed result table, never an inferred replacement from another run.

## PRJNA312093: biological-identity investigation and restricted refit

The single-end workflow finished 161/161 tasks and 20/20 technical QC checks passed. Those facts establish computational execution, **not** sample identity. The two libraries labelled adult male have VST correlation 0.800, compared with 0.943–0.994 for the other nine labelled pairs. Public run accessions, checksums, metadata and Salmon inputs were checked; no local file swap was identified. A generic BioSample shared by the libraries limits independent source-level verification.

The first diagnostic used markers selected **outside this study**: male- and female-biased adult genes from E-ERAD-478, plus stage-biased genes from PRJEB32839. A second diagnostic required stage markers to have the same direction in both sexes in PRJEB32839, reducing dependence on sex composition. Scores were standardised inside PRJNA312093 only after those marker sets had been selected; none was chosen by searching for a favourable PRJNA312093 outcome.

Under the sex-neutral stage check, `male_adult_r1` scored −3.853 versus +1.205 for `male_adult_r2`. Both libraries labelled male cercaria scored +1.165 and +1.295, while the two female cercaria libraries scored −3.619 and −3.759. The discrepancy persists under independent marker definitions. It is evidence of a **source-library expression-profile inconsistency**, not proof of a particular wet-lab event, contamination, a sample swap, or the true identity of any tube. Sample names must not be reassigned from these scores.

A sensitivity analysis then fitted DESeq2 using only 12 libraries from schistosomulum stages 1–3 (female and male, two biological replicates per group). It retained the original `~ condition` design, count filter and reporting thresholds (`padj < 0.05`, `|log2FC| >= 1`). The lowest within-group VST replicate correlation was 0.9415. Compared with the earlier 16-library refit without adults, the three stage contrasts retained 78.7%, 90.4%, and 84.8% of significant genes; shared effects had Spearman log2FC correlation at least 0.99997, with no sign reversals among shared genes. Significant-gene totals changed to 290, 1,045, and 437, so the original 20-sample tables cannot stand in for this refit.

**Admission rule:** exactly those three stage-1/2/3 contrast tables from the reviewed 12-sample refit may enter, each labelled `RESTRICTED_REFIT; TWO_REPLICATES_PER_GROUP; SOURCE_IDENTITY_LIMITATION`. The adult and cercaria contrasts remain `NOT_EVALUABLE`. The refit outputs are currently retained as private audit evidence; before materialising this decision in a published atlas, the approved tables must be packaged and checked against that audit. This is an implementation prerequisite, **not** an undecided admission vote. The public 20-sample result package remains an execution/audit record, not the approved differential-expression source.

## PRJNA494090: reason for exclusion

Read integrity and pipeline execution passed, but 10/24 libraries had Salmon mapping below the prespecified 60% review threshold. Mean mapping for selected versus control libraries differed in every passage: P6 63.06% versus 53.35%, P7 79.13% versus 62.42%, and P8 73.92% versus 55.32%. An external adult-sex signature also differed between arms, changing direction across P6/P7/P8 (+1.176, −0.883, +0.840 in standardised group-score differences). That signature is not an exact estimate of sex proportions; it is an additional check for potentially unequal biological composition.

This combination creates a credible confounding risk for interpreting DE as an isolated response to PZQ sensitivity selection. No automatic sample exclusion, post-hoc relabelling, or pipeline parameter change was made to force a favourable result. The conservative decision is to omit this study from atlas-level gene-effect comparisons while retaining its original outputs for audit or a separately designed methods investigation.

## Implementation and provenance boundary

- Show each warning adjacent to its study and contrast, including in exports; do not convert a warning into an invisible filter.
- PRJNA704646 contributes zero DE contrasts; PRJNA1150591 contributes one adult contrast; PRJNA312093 contributes three **refit** contrasts only when their approved tables are materialised. PRJNA494090 contributes none.
- Keep the original study result packages and their provenance unchanged. The private diagnostic records contain the marker definitions, per-sample scores, refit summaries and execution logs; no private server path or raw read is published here.
- Do not infer shared batch correction, causal RNA–ChIP coupling, or a pooled cross-study DE model from atlas admission.
