# RNA-seq atlas: study admission and interpretation

Decision record: 14 October 2026. This document records which study-level evidence may enter the *S. mansoni* RNA-seq atlas and why. It is **not** an atlas build, a cross-study DESeq2 analysis, or a replacement for each study's result package. The atlas remains a descriptive comparison of independently analysed studies; a contrast is interpreted only within its originating study.

`INCLUDE` means the specified evidence is scientifically approved for an atlas version. It does not assert that the atlas or every newly approved artifact has already been published. Technical workflow completion, biological interpretability, and atlas admission are distinct decisions. The canonical input for an approved contrast is its reviewed result table, never an inferred replacement from another run.

## PRJNA312093: biological-identity investigation and restricted refit

The single-end workflow finished 161/161 tasks and 20/20 technical QC checks passed. Those facts establish computational execution, **not** sample identity. The two libraries labelled adult male have VST correlation 0.800, compared with 0.943–0.994 for the other nine labelled pairs. Public run accessions, checksums, metadata and Salmon inputs were checked; no local file swap was identified. A generic BioSample shared by the libraries limits independent source-level verification.

The first diagnostic used markers selected **outside this study**: male- and female-biased adult genes from E-ERAD-478, plus stage-biased genes from PRJEB32839. A second diagnostic required stage markers to have the same direction in both sexes in PRJEB32839, reducing dependence on sex composition. Scores were standardised inside PRJNA312093 only after those marker sets had been selected; none was chosen by searching for a favourable PRJNA312093 outcome.

Under the sex-neutral stage check, `male_adult_r1` scored −3.853 versus +1.205 for `male_adult_r2`. Both libraries labelled male cercaria scored +1.165 and +1.295, while the two female cercaria libraries scored −3.619 and −3.759. The discrepancy persists under independent marker definitions. It is evidence of a **source-library expression-profile inconsistency**, not proof of a particular wet-lab event, contamination, a sample swap, or the true identity of any tube. Sample names must not be reassigned from these scores.

A sensitivity analysis then fitted DESeq2 using only 12 libraries from schistosomulum stages 1–3 (female and male, two biological replicates per group). It retained the original `~ condition` design, count filter and reporting thresholds (`padj < 0.05`, `|log2FC| >= 1`). The lowest within-group VST replicate correlation was 0.9415. Compared with the earlier 16-library refit without adults, the three stage contrasts retained 78.7%, 90.4%, and 84.8% of significant genes; shared effects had Spearman log2FC correlation at least 0.99997, with no sign reversals among shared genes. Significant-gene totals changed to 290, 1,045, and 437, so the original 20-sample tables cannot stand in for this refit.

**Admission rule:** exactly those three stage-1/2/3 contrast tables from the reviewed 12-sample refit may enter, each labelled `RESTRICTED_REFIT; TWO_REPLICATES_PER_GROUP; SOURCE_IDENTITY_LIMITATION`. If displaying TPM, use only the 12 sample columns named in `included_samples.tsv`; do not mix in the eight adult/cercaria libraries. Their sex contrasts remain `NOT_EVALUABLE`. The [approved tables](../../results/rnaseq/PRJNA312093/restricted_refit/README.md) have now been copied byte-for-byte from the private audit and checked against its SHA-256 values. The public 20-sample DE tables remain execution/audit records, not the approved differential-expression source. The atlas itself has not been rebuilt by this admission record.

## PRJNA494090: reason for exclusion

Read integrity and pipeline execution passed, but 10/24 libraries had Salmon mapping below the prespecified 60% review threshold. Mean mapping for selected versus control libraries differed in every passage: P6 63.06% versus 53.35%, P7 79.13% versus 62.42%, and P8 73.92% versus 55.32%. An external adult-sex signature also differed between arms, changing direction across P6/P7/P8 (+1.176, −0.883, +0.840 in standardised group-score differences). That signature is not an exact estimate of sex proportions; it is an additional check for potentially unequal biological composition.

This combination creates a credible confounding risk for interpreting DE as an isolated response to PZQ sensitivity selection. No automatic sample exclusion, post-hoc relabelling, or pipeline parameter change was made to force a favourable result. The conservative decision is to omit this study from atlas-level gene-effect comparisons while retaining its original outputs for audit or a separately designed methods investigation.

## PRJNA551487: whole-file QC investigation and exclusion

The paired-end workflow completed 117/117 tasks and all 20 input FASTQs passed integrity validation. Nevertheless, Salmon mapped only 12.41–15.02% of reads to the declared SM_V10/WBPS19 **mRNA** index; all ten biological libraries triggered the prespecified 60% mapping review gate. A first diagnostic based on the beginning of each FASTQ could not estimate whole-library composition. A protocol frozen before the new results specified uniform reservoir sampling of 3,000 paired reads from the **entire** verified FASTQ for each library. All ten samples covered all ten positional deciles. Two independent fresh transfers were needed after MD5 failures; their replacement files passed the frozen ENA checks before sampling.

Under the predeclared Bowtie2 local-alignment screen, 86.60–92.87% of random pairs had at least one mate compatible with a 50-kb repetitive locus of SM_V10. This is a strong **screening signal**, not a claim that 86.60–92.87% of molecules are rRNA: local alignment is permissive, the target was selected after an earlier diagnostic, and no genome-wide origin or library-preparation mechanism was proven. The [per-library public summary](../../results/rnaseq/PRJNA551487/reports/fullfile_locus_qc_summary.tsv) permits review; detailed read-level audit remains private. The original authors aligned to a different, genome-based reference, so this mRNA-only mapping percentage should not be equated with their reported genomic alignment rate.

Independently of mapping, the [primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6796685/) harvested DMSO controls from mesenteries and FPL-treated worms from livers. Thus drug and anatomical collection site are inseparable in its treatment contrast. We cannot promote that contrast as an isolated drug-response effect, and the low fraction quantified by our mRNA index also limits descriptive comparability with the atlas. The conservative decision is `EXCLUDE_FROM_ATLAS` for expression and DE while preserving technical QC, import matrices, provenance, and the original private DE output for audit. No sample was relabelled, no threshold was relaxed, and the workflow's technical `PASS` remains intact.

## PRJNA428470: coding-gene interpretation with a mapping warning

The six paired-end libraries (three adult females treated with 5-azacitidine, three controls) passed FASTQ integrity and the HelixForge workflow completed 77/77 tasks. Salmon mapping to the SM_V10/WBPS19 **mRNA** index ranged from 34.61% to 60.83%, leaving four libraries below the frozen 60% review threshold. The mean was 47.08% for treated libraries and 50.11% for controls; the low-mapping fraction was not confined to one arm. Same-group log2-CPM correlations were at least 0.9888, but good agreement among mapped genes does not identify unmapped reads.

An independent, deterministic approximately 0.5% paired-read subsample of one higher-mapping and one lower-mapping treated library reproduced the Salmon difference. STAR alignment to SM_V10 found fewer uniquely mapped and more highly multimapping reads in the lower-mapping sample; a limited rRNA-reference screen found more rRNA-compatible R1 reads. This supports a library-composition explanation in those **two** libraries, not a complete estimate for all six and not contamination by another species. Differences between the present mRNA-only reference and the published v7/WBPS14 analysis, which also included lncRNAs, have not been isolated. Two published directional examples were concordant but cannot validate the entire gene list.

To assess whether any one library drove the coding-gene contrast, six supplementary DESeq2 fits used the same `~ condition` model and count filter, each omitting one library. Relative to the frozen full fit, log2FC Spearman correlation was 0.977–0.991; no originally significant gene present in a refit changed direction, and 1,286–1,363 of the original 1,454 significant genes remained significant (88.4%–93.7%). These refits are sensitivity diagnostics, **not** replacement analyses or a reason to discard an original sample.

Admission is therefore `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` for **coding genes quantified by the declared mRNA index**, with `REVIEW_4_OF_6` retained beside the study and contrast. The 1,454 genes are exploratory statistical findings, not confirmed 5-azacitidine targets. No inference is made about lncRNAs, absent transcripts, or the unquantified read fraction. A dated private audit supplement preserves the method, per-omission results, R environment and logs; the public study package records the decision without publishing private server paths.

## Implementation and provenance boundary

- Show each warning adjacent to its study and contrast, including in exports; do not convert a warning into an invisible filter.
- PRJNA704646 and PRJNA1177852 contribute zero DE contrasts; PRJNA1150591 and PRJNA428470 contribute one each; PRJNA312093 contributes three verified **restricted-refit** contrasts. PRJNA494090 and PRJNA551487 contribute neither expression nor DE effects to the atlas.
- Keep the original study result packages and their provenance unchanged. The private diagnostic records contain the marker definitions, per-sample scores, refit summaries and execution logs; no private server path or raw read is published here.
- Do not infer shared batch correction, causal RNA–ChIP coupling, or a pooled cross-study DE model from atlas admission.
