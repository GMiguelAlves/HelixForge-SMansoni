Interpretation boundaries

  • TPM supports descriptive cross-study displays, not count-based inference.
  • DESeq2 effects are never recomputed or pooled across studies.
  • Anderson, Wang, Vasconcelos and Almeida are external publication evidence; none adds a bulk sample, atlas TPM value or atlas DESeq2 result.
  • Vasconcelos v5.2 coordinates are not displayed as SM_V10 coordinates.
  • Curated historical candidate IDs use the explicit v2 mapping, including sequence-based and functional-successor evidence; other historical literature IDs require an exact or unique WBPS19 stable-ID match. All source records remain in the downloadable tables.
  • RNA–ChIP links are stage-aware associations between independent experiments, not sample-level correlations or causal evidence.
  • A missing ChIP assay or linked peak is not evidence that a mark is biologically absent.
  • not tested, not significant, adjusted p-value unavailable, and gene absent remain distinct.
  • PRJNA602528 has one sample per time point and remains descriptive.
  • Batch metadata are retained, but formal assessment is deferred.
