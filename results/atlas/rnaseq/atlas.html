Interpretation boundaries

  • TPM supports descriptive cross-study displays, not count-based inference.
  • DESeq2 effects are never recomputed or pooled across studies.
  • not tested, not significant, and gene absent remain distinct.
  • PRJNA602528 has one sample per time point and remains descriptive.
  • Batch metadata are retained, but formal assessment is deferred.
