Experimental design & processing

i
Descriptive HP1 regional candidates. MACS3 calls are unchanged; nf-core/chipseq 2.1.0 merges overlapping or bookended intervals, retaining candidates with at least one calling library. Single-library and multiple-library evidence remain distinct; shared core requires at least two libraries and does not crop candidate regions. HOMER 4.11 assigns the nearest promoter, including for intergenic regions; this is not proof of regulation. Gene-assignment ambiguity is not assessed because these source executions predate the TSS snapshot. Stage and strain are confounded (cercaria SmVEN; sporocyst SmDFO); biological independence of deposited libraries is not established. FRiP is 0.201–3.161%; the 200 bp extension is operational. Occupancy significance and differential binding were not assessed.
