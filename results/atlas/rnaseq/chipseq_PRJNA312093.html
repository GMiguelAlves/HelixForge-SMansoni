Scope, QC and interpretation

Published descriptive regional catalogs: H3K27me3 broad and H3K4me3 narrow; two deposited IP libraries per sex/stage/mark, with matched Input IDs. This compact publication is not certification of complete upstream QC or a combined terminal run. DB remains HOLD: adult sex/instrument confounding and unresolved source-pool relationships. H3K27me3 strict direct comparison failed for one nearest-gene assignment in each male group (shared TSS, Smp_095580/Smp_341610); original assignments retained and ambiguity not assessed. H3K4me3 preserves assessed and unassessed ambiguity, and IDR <=0.05 is separate evidence, not the annotation catalog. No regulation or occupancy significance is inferred. BAMs, individual peak coordinates and bigWig signals are not published in this compact package. Adult/cercaria RNA from this accession remain excluded; use compatible approved RNA studies without sample pairing.

Experimental design

Female and male adults and cercariae; two deposited IP libraries per sex / stage / mark, with matched Input identifiers. H3K27me3 uses broad calls; H3K4me3 uses narrow calls. Library labels alone do not certify independence.

IDR is ranked by signal value at 0.05; it does not certify every candidate region. Genomic coordinates and per-library covered bases are preserved. Individual-call coordinates and bigWigs are not in this compact public package; their absence is not a negative result.
